# AX-7: books and bookcases made real

Scope: the AX programme's A7 rows (`.agents/gaps/ARCHITECT_GAP_ANALYSIS.md` Bookcase, Small bookcase): vanilla 1.5 core books end to end, ENG-003 wiki numbers, original names, prose and titles.

## Behaviour
- **Books** (`domain/economy/book`): novel, textbook and schematic are items (one per stack) with a quality and `item.Stack.Book`, a seed that fixes:
  - an original generated title (our own word lists);
  - the skill or two (25 %) a textbook teaches;
  - the open research project or two (25 %) a schematic covers.
- **Minting.** Every new book is minted in `SpawnStack` from a hash of the colony seed and the stack id, with no dice drawn. Non-books keep `Book == 0`, so their hash is unchanged. `SameLoad` never joins two books into one load.
- **Reading** (`game/books`, job.Read).
  - In free time, a colonist below Entertained reads a random loose book within 40 tiles on one free time in two.
  - It picks the book up and sits on the best free seat within 12 tiles; a room with a higher reading bonus counts as nearer. With no seat, it reads standing.
  - A read lasts an hour (`ReadTicks`), or ends sooner when recreation is full; a forced read always lasts the hour. Afterwards the book is put down and haulers shelve it.
  - If work replaces the read (`startJob`), the reader puts the book down.
- **Gains per hour (wiki), spread exactly over the hour:**
  - Recreation: 36 % times the power. Novels by quality are x1.2, 1.4, 1.6, 1.8, 2.0, 2.25 and 2.5; textbooks and schematics are x1.0.
  - Intellectual: 250 XP for any book.
  - Textbook: 125 to 500 XP for one skill, or 78 to 312 each for two, only below the quality's cap (levels 2, 5, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16).
  - Schematic: 20 to 80 points for one project, or 12 to 50 each for two, divided by the colony's cost percent as bench work is. Finishing a project posts the letter and keeps another pick (`Lab.Study`).
  - The room's reading bonus multiplies everything except the Intellectual XP.
- **Bookcases.**
  - `zone.KindBookcase` storage holds books only (`Universe`) and 5 books per cell (`BookcaseStacks`): 10 in the bookcase, 5 in the small one. It runs at Preferred priority.
  - Reading bonus (wiki): +0.8 % for each of the first 5 books, +0.4 % for books 6-10, +0.2 % for books 11-15, and +0.1 % after that, capped at +20 %. Only books shelved in an indoor room's bookcases count.
  - The bonus multiplies reading and that room's research bench factor (`research.Rooms.ReadingBonus`).
  - The shelf search and `cellFull` now read `zone.Kind.Stacks()`.
- **Sources** (no crafting in core 1.5):
  - The bulk hauler stocks novels and textbooks. The orbital curio merchant stocks all three, after the mind core.
  - 35 % of ruin sheds hold one book, generated by mapgen's own `ruin_books` stream into `Ruin.Books`, which no mapgen golden hashes.
  - A dead raider leaves one 4 % of the time, hashed, unclaimed and forbidden outside home.
  - Chances and counts are original tuning.
- **Order.** The float menu shows "Read <title>" (`OrderRead`); the item pane has a Read button.

## UI
- **Item pane** (`inspector/ax7.go`): the title is the book's title plus its quality, the subtitle its kind. The lines give recreation power, what it teaches or researches per hour (and the level cap) at its quality, and the Intellectual XP.
- **Bookcase pane:** the reading bonus line, then each shelved book with its kind and quality. It has a Storage button (the books filter).
- **Pawn pane:** "Reading: <title>" (`PawnView.AX.Reading`).
- **Architect:** one card family, `bookcase` then `small_bookcase` (`ToolDef.Family`).
- **Render:** AR3's keys. Bookcases are drawn empty, half or full by stored books (`render/world/book_keys.go`). Books use `item/novel|textbook|schematic`, and the icons are `ui_icon/bookcase|small_bookcase|novel`.
- **Views:** `ItemView.AX.Title`, `Teaches` and `Power`; `BuildingView.AX.ReadingBonus`; `PawnView.AX.Reading`.
