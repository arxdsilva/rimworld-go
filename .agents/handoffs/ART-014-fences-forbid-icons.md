# ART-014 Fences, gates, forbidden overlay and icons

## What changed
- **New family file** `assets/source/blender/artlib/families/fence.py`.
- **Fences:** wood and stone, 16 autotile masks each (`fence_<mat>_00..15`) with normal maps.
- **Gates:** `fence_gate_<facing>` closed and `fence_gate_open_<facing>` for 4 facings, with normals.
- **Overlay:** `world/overlay/forbidden` (red marker) with normal map.
- **UI icons:** `order_forbid`, `order_allow`, `fence`, `fence_gate`, `overlay_rooms`.
- All art is original and generated by the Blender pipeline.

## Verification
- `make art` including validation; the validator needs a Python with Pillow (`/usr/local/bin/python3`).
- Tests: renderer `art_test.go` asset checks.

## Known limitations
- No stone gate art; there is a single gate kind.
