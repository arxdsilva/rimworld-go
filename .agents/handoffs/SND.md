## SND-001-audio-runtime
Audio runtime · 2026-10-21 · 5f0d1f6df
- Changed: the game has sound. The UI adapter decides what is heard and sends it through a `Sound` port it owns; new outbound adapter `internal/adapters/outbound/ebitenaudio` plays it. `AppConfig.Sound` nil means silent (tests, `-capture`, `-noaudio`, `RIMWORLD_GO_NOAUDIO=1`; no audio device opened). Code in `ebiten/sound.go`, `sound_signals.go`, `sound_director.go`.
- Decisions: only `ebitenaudio` imports `ebiten/v2/audio`; `cmd/simulate` never depends on audio (`arch_test.go`). Cues come from presentation signals only: strikes from `workStrikeAt` (paused clock silent, 3x speed strikes 3x as often), world diffs (frames finished, trees cut, mines done, door occupancy), traces, lightning (thunder 20-90 frames later), new threats and notices, clicks and speed keys. Sound reads presentation state only and uses its own RNG; no determinism impact.
- Placement: pan up to +-0.8 by screen x; fade to silence half a view off screen. Camera distance (`zoomEar`): effects fade from full at zoom 1.5 to silence at the map's zoom-out limit (`minZoomFor`), equal steps per halving; stereo narrows to half width and copies thin out (3, 2, 1 per frame); campfires fade with them; beds, music, UI and thunder don't.
- Limits: 4 frames between plays of one id; 3 copies of an id per frame; 4 nearest cues at the fastest speed; 24 engine voices (oldest stolen). A missing id is silent and logged once, never panics.
- Ambience: `amb.day`/`amb.night` crossfade at dawn and dusk (no crickets in winter or at 8 C or below); `amb.rain` follows rain and storms; x0.4 when the camera centre is indoors; the 2 nearest burning fires crackle in stereo; the menu plays a low night bed and the campfire. Music: menu (calm until a menu theme exists); calm with 40-150 s silence between plays; tension during a raid and 45 s after (once a tension track exists); x0.6 under the pause menu; 2.5 s crossfades.
- Animal voices (SND-003 contract): ids `animal.<roster key>.<event>` (events `call`, `attack`, `hurt`, `die`) plus `animal.burst`. Fallback via `Sound.Has` (`Renderer.soundHas`): species, then group (`small_mammal`, `large_mammal`, `canid`, `felid`, `bovid`, `bird_small`, `bird_large`, `reptile`, `insect`; `voiceGroup` in `animal_sounds.go`), then `animal.generic.<event>`. Calls: per-animal timers 25-70 s scaled by game speed, none while paused, on screen only, capped 2 calls/s and 3 species per 10 s; a cry once per flee; attack/hurt from combat traces with `sfx.melee_hit` kept under at 0.6; die when the animal leaves the world and a corpse of its species is on its tile; burst from `WorldView.Bursts`. OGG one-shots decode once into an LRU (24 MiB PCM), panned like WAV.
- Limits: no pitch or filter control in Ebitengine (variety from 3 pre-made variants; indoor effect is volume only); OGG voices not panned (music and beds not positional).
- Open: listening in the running game not done; the device smoke test is behind `-tags audiodevice`.

New `pkg/sfxsynth` (synthesis, seeded dither, WAV, loop crossfade), `pkg/audiomanifest` (sound list format and validation), `cmd/audiogen` (`recipes.go`, `-check`, `-loopfix`, `-looping`, `-register`), `assets/metadata/audio/audio.json`, `assets/source/audio/prompts.json`, `assets/source/audio/elevenlabs/ledger.json`, `scripts/audio_import.sh`, `.mcp.json`/`.cursor/mcp.json` `elevenlabs` server (`ELEVEN_LABS_API_KEY` mapped to `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`), Makefile `audio`, `audio-check`.
- Decisions: effects are byte-identical across runs and machines (`TestGenerateIsDeterministicAndKeepsOtherSources`); regenerating keeps every non-procedural entry. ElevenLabs rules: measure real cost with `check_subscription` first, at most one retry per sound, every take in the ledger, a person approves each take before import. Spending order within a 10,000-credit budget: calm music, day bed, night bed, rain, tension, menu theme, optional second calm track. Campfire and thunder are code-made (no credits).
- Update 2026-10-21: ambience beds (day, night, rain) from ElevenLabs free tier, each four 5 s clips stitched into a 16 s seamless loop at about -20 LUFS, 600 credits (about 50 per clip; API counter lagged and showed 300), 12 takes in the ledger, credited on the Credits screen and in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (non-commercial; replaced by SND-005). - Fix: `scripts/audio_import.sh` measures loudness and applies the exact gain with a limiter (single-pass loudnorm undershot short, quiet clips by 2-8 dB). Totals then: 32 sounds, 10.2 MB of 20 MB.
- Open: restart the editor so it inherits the key and approve `elevenlabs` in `/mcp` (obsolete); add the music/ambience credit line (done for ambience).

## SND-004-moss-backend
MOSS-SoundEffect backend for creature voices · 2026-11-01 · d203170b5
- Decision (user, 2026-10-31): "switch creature voices to MOSS if it makes them better". `cmd/sfxgen` gets `-backend moss` (default), running the open-source MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0 locally and processing each take at once; `-backend elevenlabs` keeps the paid path and its spending rules. The four mechanoids (harrow, spindle, bulwark, quill) get 16 MOSS voices and their own `mech` voice group (`render/cue/animal_sounds.go`; no group sounds, so a missing mech sound falls to generic) replacing the insect stand-in.
- Runner: `cmd/sfxgen/moss.go` + `assets/source/tools/moss/generate.py` (`--steps 100 --cfg 4 --device auto`; torch dynamo off since MPS cannot compile float64 kernels; MPS float64/complex128 patches; 24-bit WAV). Seeds: stable hash of id and take number, or `-moss-seed`. Memory gate: waits until macOS `vm_stat` shows 24 GB free+inactive (`-moss-min-free-gb`, `-moss-poll`, `-moss-max-wait`); one take at a time. Paths default to `$XDG_CACHE_HOME` or `~/.cache/rimworld-go/moss/{venv,MOSS-SoundEffect-v2.0}`, overridable by `-moss-python`/`-moss-model` or `$SFXGEN_MOSS_PYTHON`/`$SFXGEN_MOSS_MODEL`. No model, venv or cache in the repo; `assets/source/audio/moss/raw/` gitignored.
- Ledger: every take records `backend` (296 older takes backfilled `elevenlabs`); MOSS takes record `seed`; a free take has no retry cap. A take failing processing (e.g. silent) is logged, the run continues and exits with an error naming the ids so the user can `-reject` and rerun. `-approve` writes `source: moss`, tool, seed and the Apache-2.0 license line; a generated entry (ElevenLabs or MOSS) can replace another without `-replace` (`audiomanifest.Generated`, `SourceMOSS`). The review page has a backend column. `cmd/audiogen -animals` no longer overwrites approved MOSS takes.
- Mech voices: 11 takes from the 2026-10-29 trial; 5 regenerated 2026-10-31 with tighter prompts and new seeds (`harrow.hurt` -29.7 to -18.0 LUFS, `harrow.die` -23.9 to -19.0, `spindle.hurt` 0.5 s to 1.0 s at -20.2 peak-limited, `quill.hurt` -28.0 to -18.0, `quill.die` -23.2 to -19.0). Prompt rows in `animal_prompts.json` carry the MOSS prompt cores within A3 ranges. Approved with `reviewed_by: "agent V (metrics checked; user to listen)"`: nobody has listened. `make audio-check`: 403 sounds, 24.5 MB of 30 MB. Tests: `cmd/sfxgen/moss_test.go` (backend selection, command line, dry run, preflight, retry rules, memory wait, `vm_stat` parsing, ledger paths).
- Open: user to listen to the 16 mech voices (`sfxgen -review -only 'animal.harrow.*,animal.spindle.*,animal.bulwark.*,animal.quill.*' -o page.html`) and the A/B page (timber wolf, chicken, hive crawler, MOSS vs ElevenLabs, scratchpad, not committed; no ElevenLabs voice replaced then; resolved by SND-005); trial mech takes run longer than the A3 lengths (regenerating shorter is free); `oggenc` (Homebrew `vorbis-tools`) needed by `-process`. ## SND-005-moss-replaces-elevenlabs
MOSS replaces every ElevenLabs sound · 2026-11-06 · dc7fcb099
- Decision: ElevenLabs free-tier sounds were non-commercial only and blocked an open licence, so all are replaced by locally generated MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0 takes: 281 animal voices (61 species, 9 groups, hive burst; 491 files with variants) and the three ambience beds (day, night, rain; 20 s takes, seed 7, 100 steps, same prompts). The user approved every take after the review page and asked for the ambience to ship. No ElevenLabs output ships; `make audio-check` keeps it so.
- Tooling: `sfxgen -supersede elevenlabs` makes approved ElevenLabs sounds again; the ledger records diffusion steps per take; `generate.py` `crop()` cuts a short sound from its onset when the model starts it late in its 30 s window (on-time takes bit-identical); `-approve` of an ElevenLabs take needs `-license` with the paid plan's terms (free-tier constant gone). `pkg/audiomanifest` `Validate` (hence `Decode`, `-approve`, `-register`, `make audio-check`) rejects a license containing "non-commercial" (`NonCommercial` exported, `TestValidateRejects`). `cmd/audiogen -register -seed N` with `scripts/audio_import.sh ... <seed>` lists an imported MOSS take as `source: moss`, seed, no credits; a take from another backend replaces the old files (`cmd/audiogen/register_test.go`).
- Assets: `assets/metadata/audio/audio.json` has 300 MOSS entries and no ElevenLabs entries; ledger keeps every MOSS take approved by arxdsilva and the old ElevenLabs takes as a record (history keeps the old files under their own terms). Hurt prompts for `hare`, `cougar`, `reptile`, `fennec_fox` reworded ("... loudly in pain") because the earlier wording produced only a click. UI: Credits screen (`mainmenu.CreditsLines`), `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `assets/AGENTS.md`, `docs/FEATURES.md`, `docs/DEVELOPING.md`, ART §25.1, skill `rimworld-go-creatures`, `audio_import.sh` header now name MOSS.
- Generation notes: 50 diffusion steps (`-moss-steps 50`), ~105 s per take (100 steps ~230 s, same sound). The pipeline always denoises 30 s; shorter windows failed (10 s silent, 3 s noise). A second parallel process gave no speed-up (GPU saturated).
- Limits: `make audio-check` 403 sounds, 24.7 MB of 30 MB.
- Open: the project licence itself (GPL-3.0 code, CC BY-SA 4.0 assets) comes next.
