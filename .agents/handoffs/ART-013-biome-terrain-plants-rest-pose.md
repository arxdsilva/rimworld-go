# ART-013 Biome terrain, plants, items and rest pose

(ART-012 is the creature-shadows handoff; this one takes the next number.)

## What changed
- **Terrain:** sand, marsh and ice, 4 variants each plus 15 edge masks (`families/terrain.py`).
- **Plants:** pine (with snow looks), jungle tree, cactus (with dry), shrub (with dry), reeds, 3 variants each (`families/plant.py`).
- **Furniture and items:** feeding trough (empty/full, 4 facings), wool carpet; items milk, wool, egg, fertile egg, ember oil, wool parka, wool carpet roll; matching UI icons.
- **Animals:** a rest pose (`_rest`) for all 64 animal sets, including female sets; shown with snore Zs in the renderer.
- **Biome icons:** 12 under `assets/generated/ui/icons/biome_*.png`. Palette `assets/palettes/materials_biomes.yaml`.
- **Renderer** (`biome_art.go`, `grass_draw.go`): grass tint by biome; juveniles are scaled from the adult art, not separate sprites.
- All art is original and generated by the Blender pipeline.

## Verification
- `make art` including validation; the validation step needs a Python with Pillow (`/usr/local/bin/python3`).
- Tests: `art_test.go`, `biome_ui_test.go`, `animal_ui_test.go`.

## Known limitations
- Worn wool parka reuses the leather parka layer, tinted.
- No dedicated juvenile art.
- Pine snow looks are variants only; no seasonal cactus or reed states beyond dry.
