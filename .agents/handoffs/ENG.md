- Limits: gate used the `-fpsprobe` grown colony (seed 1) at farthest zoom, not a hand-played session; first-use pawn tint bakes cost frames in the first ~25 s at a new zoom (a pre-warm would remove them). Slow ground only adds path cost on crowd repaths (SIM-033). Planet rebuild on load (plan §5.8) and worker-goroutine save snapshot (§6.8) not benchmarked. `BenchmarkReadModel` 47 ns when cached.

## ENG-004-play019-ram-graphics-crops
RAM-aware graphics defaults, memory diet, crops on their tile (PLAY-019) · 2026-10-25 · 138bc8bbe
- Decisions (user, 2026-10-25): parallel work checks RAM before spawning agents; the game checks RAM and picks smooth graphics defaults unless the player overrides, warns only and never blocks; hardware detected at first launch only, no runtime quality changes; crops stay on their own tile.
- Changed: `cmd/rambudget` (`make agent-budget`) reads free RAM (`internal/platform/sysinfo`) and prints how many heavy/art/light agents fit after a 4 GiB reserve (PARALLEL_WORK §1/§3/§5, skill step 0; agents test with `GOFLAGS=-p=2` and targeted `-run`). Memory diet: art indexed and decoded on first use, background prewarm keeps deflated copies (~46 MiB, all families except animals), CPU pixels freed after GPU upload (crop units, tilled overlays, grass tufts keep theirs, `cpuReadKeys`), tints bake on GPU in a 128 MiB LRU.
- Changed (UI): settings v5 adds Quality, Shadows, Lighting, Water, Grass, Detected, Chosen. First start (or first after an update) picks High (≥12 GB and 6 cores), Medium (≥8 GB) or Low, keeps rows the player changed. Options › Graphics: Quality (Custom once a row changes), Detect again, four detail rows applied live (shadows off skips cast layers; lighting off = flat colour scale; simple water = baked frames; grass keeps 60 % or 30 % of tufts). `-graphics low|medium|high` for one run. RAM estimate 1850 MiB + 6600 B/tile; main-menu notice below 6 GB (`Gameplay.RAMNoticeSeen`); sizes over 70 % of RAM warn in picker and site panel.
- Changed: crops root at the art's tile-centre point (`origin.y + 64`); `cropFit` moves a unit south (root ≤0.95) then shrinks to its tile; sway pivots at the root, scale clamp 0.35–2.0. `TestCropUnitsStayOnTheirTile` covers every crop/stage/pattern/LOD (101,183 units failed at the old anchor).
- Result (2560x1440 Mac, `-fpsprobe 15`): max RSS 2.4–2.7 GB → ~1.05 GB; 250 map 1% low 9.0 → 86.6 fps; grown 500 1% low 56.0 → 83.9. A hidden/locked window throttles the probe to ~9 fps.
- Open: lighting off still routes LOD ground/blood/dirt/crop batches through `lit.kage` with flat light; animals not prewarmed (4608 sprites, ~765 MiB decoded, cold fills in heavy raids; pawn atlas holds 204 MiB deflated, smaller `cmd/atlasgen` pages would cut it); tall crops show fewer plants per tile, 4-unit pattern west column overhangs ~0.08 tile; no low-RAM notice on the boot screen; estimate constants from one Mac (re-measure on Windows/Linux). `TestAtlasCoversKeys` known local failure (git-ignored pawn PNGs older than the atlas).
