## Corrections found by the analysis

The fragments and the shots corrected the plan (Part D) in these places. The rows carry the corrected text; this list says what changed.

**Model**
- Five Core kinds accept themselves: ore lump (30 d), item stash (16 d), downed refugee (10 d), peace talks (no timer shown) and prisoner rescue (23 d) (Q09-Q17). Three are offers with an accept window: hostile camp (6 d, then 17 d after accepting, Q00-Q08), threatened joiner (7 h, no timer after, Q20-Q23) and trade request (4.6 d, then 16 d, Q24, Q32).
- The reward is chosen at accept, through one "Accept for:" row per option (Q02-Q04, Q22, Q24). `Accept(q, now, choice)` is right.
- A dismissed quest goes to Historical, can still be accepted from there, and comes back with Restore (Q27-Q31).
- An expired offer sends no letter; its detail says it expired (Q36). A failure sends a letter that names the cause, with a "view quest" row (Q38-Q39).
- Failure on a hostile giver is per kind: turning the giver hostile failed the trade request, but not the item stash or downed refugee from the same faction (Q38-Q40). `Def.FailOnGiverHostile` decides.
- 5,500 is not a cap: random picks stop at 5,500 and the rest of the budget is paid in filler items (gold, plasteel, uranium).
- The cadence is one offer per 10-day window, so gaps reach 19.9 days; "never closer than the interval" is the wrong invariant.
- Goodwill plus items is allowed for the hostile camp (Q02-Q04 show goodwill + camp loot).
- Factions are `int` indexes (saved as 1 + index); rewards are `trade.Line`s, not world `item.Stack`s.
- The negotiation odds have 6 published anchors, not 2.
- Relation changes are detected by an hourly per-faction snapshot, not a helper at 14 `faction.Adjust` callers.

## The quest model

**States.** Offered → Active → Succeeded | Failed. Offered → Expired (accept window ran out). Offered → Active at once for self-accepting kinds. Terminal states never change. `Dismissed` is a flag that hides a quest under Historical; it changes no state or timer.

**Timers.** `AcceptBy` (offers only) and `Deadline` (0 = none). `Def.DeadlineOutcome` is Fail for every Core kind. All timers live in one due-heap of `(tick, id, event)`; the hourly step pops only due entries.

**Generator.** One offer per 10-day window from landing (none before day 6), at a rolled tick in the window. A kind is picked by weight among those whose `CanOffer(ctx)` holds. Points = one `difficulty.ThreatPoints` call over all colony maps and caravans, × stars (1-3) × a 700-1300 permille roll, applied after the floor and with no cap. A lost window is not caught up.

**Parts.** A quest is a list of tagged parts: Site, Pawn, Items, Threat, Delivery, Talk. Kinds react to typed signals: site census, pawn joined, freed or died, caravan arrived, map closed, relation changed.

**Rewards.** 1 option or 3. Kinds: Items (`[]trade.Line`), Pawn (a seed, previewed), Goodwill. Goodwill is alone except for the hostile camp. Budget = points de-scaled by threat scale × `QuestRewardPct`; random lines up to 5,500, filler for the rest. `Def.RewardAt` OnAccept or OnSuccess, and `Quest.Paid`: a paid reward is never taken back. `Def.Delivery`: Home (pod), IntoCaravan, AtSite.

**Failure.** Deadline, giver turned hostile (per kind), target no longer hostile, kind-specific signals (pawn died, site lost). No goodwill penalty on expiry, trash or failure (none published).

## Phases

Every row names its phase. A row found later goes into the right phase before that phase starts.

### QST-1: contract (lead, D-dom, D-app)

**Step 0: budget relief** (lead; one commit per move)
- `G/letters.go` moves to `G/letters/` with a `Host` role; `letters.State` replaces `letterState` in place (no new `Game` field). Letters gain `Actions []Action{ID, Label, Cmd}`, which replace the `Join`/`Demand` bools; the v15 migration maps the bools to actions. `letterMemo` compares a version counter, not `slices.Equal` (QST-155..159).
- The scheduled-incident queue (`G/story.go:314-343`) moves to `G/storyq/` the same way (QST-161).
- Gate: existing letter and incident tests, `TestSaveSchema` (type paths refreshed, names and kinds unchanged).

**Step 1: `internal/domain/quest`** (D-dom, opus). Pure: no RNG source, no clock; rolls and ticks are arguments.
- Types: `ID uint32`, `KindID` (our own ids), `State` {Offered, Active, Succeeded, Failed, Expired}, `Reason` (append-only: Timeout, GiverHostile, TargetNotHostile, PawnLost, SiteLost, …), `Quest` {ID, Kind, State, Reason, Giver, Target int32 (−1 none), Stars, Points, OfferedAt, AcceptBy, Deadline, EndedAt, Parts, Rewards, Chosen, Paid, Dismissed}, tagged `Part`s, `RewardOption` {Kind Items|Pawn|Goodwill, Value, Lines []trade.Line, PawnSeed, Goodwill}.
- `Def` {ID, Weight, CanOffer, CanAccept, AutoAccept, Accept/Duration windows, DeadlineOutcome, RewardAt, Delivery, Violent, GoodwillPlusItems, FailOnGiverHostile}. `Defs()` is a literal built by the caller (no `init()`, no package var).
- Transitions `Accept(q, now, choice)`, `Expire`, `Succeed`, `Fail`, `Dismiss`, `Restore` return `(Quest, error)`; a dismissed offer can still be accepted.
- Rules: `Due`, `FailOnRelation`, `ThreatPoints(points, stars, permille)`, `RewardBudget`, `PickRewards` (5,500 random then filler), `Negotiate(social, permille)` over 6 anchors, `Cadence` (one per 10-day window, none before day 6), `Weight` with the population curve.
- Tests: transition tables, `FuzzQuestTransitions`, reward invariants (budget met, goodwill alone except the camp, 1 or 3 options), cadence (exactly one offer per window, gaps ≤ 19.9 d), negotiation odds ±0.5 % at each anchor over 200k rolls, a 2-year pure generator determinism run.

**Step 2: `G/quest/`** (D-app, opus; must not import `G`). The `Quests` root: quests sorted by id, a due-heap, `Hourly(now)` pops due entries and diffs the per-faction relation snapshot, `Daily(now)` runs the cadence, `Signal(s)`, `Apply(cmd)`, `View()`, `Save()`/`Load()`. Roles in `deps.go`, each ≤ 8 methods: Clock, World, Sites, Pawns, Items, Threats, Letters, Goodwill, Rolls. Kinds are passed into `quest.New(host, roles, kinds...)`.

**Step 3: game glue** (lead). `quest_host.go` (adapter types, `care_host.go` style); root in `worldState` via `questOf(g)`; `Hourly` from `phaseWorld`, `Daily` from `dayRollover`; signals from `maps` census, `Fleet.Arrived`, joins and deaths; commands appended to `command.Kind` (`AcceptQuest`, `DismissQuest`, `RestoreQuest`, `FulfilRequest`, `Negotiate`, `SetRewardPref`) and `OrderFreePrisoner` (with `TargetsPawn` as a switch); `WorldView.Quests` with a version memo; `maps.Meta.Quest`, `Request.Quest`, `Request.Layout`; difficulty `QuestRewardPct` and `QuestThreats`; `streamQuest`; `SaveVersion` 15 with its migration. The game package's ceilings in `internal/arch/solid_budget.txt` are lowered to the new counts.

**Step 4: tests.** `G/quest` against fakes (~90 % coverage); `TestQuestOfferExpiresAndLeavesHistory`, `TestQuestStateHashStableAcrossSaveLoad`, `TestSaveV14LoadsWithoutQuests`, `BenchmarkQuestHourly1000` (flat in quest count).
