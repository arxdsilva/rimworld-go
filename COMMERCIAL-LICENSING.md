The code is free under the [GNU General Public License, version 3](LICENSE). You may use, study, change and share it at no cost.

The condition: if you distribute anything built on rimworld-go (a game, a fork, a tool), you must also ship its **full source code** under GPL-3.0, so your players can rebuild and change it. Building it, changing it and playing it yourself, without distributing it, needs no further license.

The art and audio are not under the GPL. See [`ASSETS-LICENSE.md`](ASSETS-LICENSE.md).

## The commercial option: ship closed-source

To ship a game built on rimworld-go **without** publishing your source code, you need a commercial license.

**Contact:** arxdsilva@gmail.com

### Pricing

Each deal depends on the size of the studio and its expected revenue. The model for a small studio is:

- a small one-time fee, or none;
- a percentage of gross revenue, paid only once the game has earned more than a set threshold.

For example: no upfront fee, then 4% of gross revenue after the first US$50,000. Write to arxdsilva@gmail.com for an offer for your studio.

### Contributing back

Studios that contribute features back to rimworld-go can get a lower royalty. The reduction is agreed in each deal. Every contribution needs a signed [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) first.

### What every commercial license includes

- **The usage data package stays in.** Your game keeps rimworld-go's usage data reporting (`internal/adapters/outbound/usagehttp`) switched on and working. Details, including what is reported, the players' opt-out and privacy notices, are agreed in each license.
- **No use of the name.** You may not use the name "rimworld-go", or anything that suggests a connection to RimWorld or Ludeon Studios, in your game's title, store pages or marketing.
- **Assets are separate.** The art and audio are licensed only if your agreement names them.
