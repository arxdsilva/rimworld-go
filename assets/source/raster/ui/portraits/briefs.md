# Storyteller portrait briefs

Provenance for `steady.png`, `hearth.png` and `gambler.png` in this folder
(ART §17.1). The busts were made on 2026-10-26 with Canva's image generator
from the prompts below. The only reference image was our own original
main-menu colonist (`../main_menu_colonist.png`), used for rendering style
only. No RimWorld art, storyteller or otherwise, was used as a reference or
input.

The sources keep their flat magenta background. `make portraits`
(`assets/source/tools/portrait_cut.py`) keys it out and writes the 512×640
busts, the 120×150 thumbnails and their metadata. Don't hand-edit
`generated/ui/portraits/` or `metadata/ui_portrait/`.

## Shared style block

> ORIGINAL character portrait for a sci-fi frontier colony game. Match the
> rendering style of the reference image (heroic comic illustration, bold
> black cel outlines, flat two-tone cel shading, slight painterly texture)
> but NOT its character. Chest-up bust, three-quarter view, head turned
> slightly, eyes toward the viewer, warm rim light from upper left. Muted
> earthy palette. Background: solid flat pure magenta (#FF00FF), no gradient,
> no shadow on the background. No text, no logo, no frame. Original design.

## steady: Orla Vane, the Steady Hand

> A woman in her late 50s, close-cropped silver hair, weathered calm face
> with crow's feet, a calm knowing half-smile. A dispatcher's radio headset
> hangs around her neck with the mic boom folded down. Quilted navy work
> jacket with one reflective hi-vis yellow stripe across the chest, collar
> turned up. A clipboard tucked under one arm.

## hearth: Bram Holloway, the Hearthkeeper

> A broad, stocky man in his 60s with a ruddy face, a short neat white
> beard, friendly crinkled eyes, mid-chuckle. Rust-red knitted cardigan worn
> over a canvas work apron. He holds a steaming enamel camp mug in both
> hands close to his chest.

## gambler: Kestrel Quay, the Gambler

> An angular, lean person in their 30s with asymmetric dark hair, one side
> of the head shaved short, one eyebrow raised, a sly confident grin. Long
> wine-red coat with a high collar, brass buttons and a dark lining. One
> hand raised holding a single blank playing card between two fingers, card
> face toward the viewer with no symbols.
